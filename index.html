01Who it's for

For the practitioner standing at the edge of going public.

I'm Leah. I built my own coaching practice from scratch — and I know what it feels like to sit on work you believe in, not quite ready to put it out there. The overthinking, the almost-launching, the gap between knowing what you do and being able to say it publicly. I've been in that place.

Now, alongside my work in tech and coaching, I run a small studio building websites for people whose hardest part isn't the technical execution — it's crossing that threshold. If you've been certified for a year and still don't have a site. If you've been seeing clients privately and you're ready to make it public. If you keep almost-launching and not quite. This is for you.

03How it goes

Four parts. One conversation at a time.

01

Clarity

Before anything gets built, we figure out what you actually want to say. This is coaching, not just intake — we talk about your work, your people, and where the stuckness lives.

a A 30-minute discovery call to meet each other and see where you are in your launch
b A short questionnaire and a shared page where you gather inspiration — sites you love, images that speak to you
c A 90-minute session where we go deep — who your client is, what you're trying to build, and what's been keeping you from doing it

This step is also available on its own, for practitioners who want help getting clear before they're ready to build.

02

Form

The work starts to take shape. I go away with everything from our clarity session and come back with options — then we plan the bones of the site together.

a A handful of aesthetic directions for us to review — palette, typography, feel
b A page-planning session to map out what you need and how it all fits together
c Logo and visual identity direction, partnered with designers I trust when something custom is needed
03

Build

I build the site. You can write your own content, we can write it together, or I can write it for you. Then we walk through it page by page and refine until it feels right.

a Content — written by you, by me, or together as a creative process
b A first version of the site, built custom and clean enough that an engineer could pick it up later if you grow
c One or two rounds of review — we go through every page together and make it yours
04

Launch

We sign off, pick a date, and put your work into the world. After that, I'm here if you need me — small updates, fixes, the things that come up as your practice grows.

a Final sign-off and launch day
b An optional monthly retainer for ongoing care — bug fixes, small edits, keeping things current
c New pages or bigger changes scoped and priced as they come up
