The Launch by Leah Client Questionnaire
Step 1 · Clarity

Before we build.

This is the beginning of the work — and it's not about your website yet. It's about getting clear on what you want to say, who you're saying it to, and what's been keeping you from putting it out there.

Take your time with these questions. There are no wrong answers. Write what feels true, even if it's messy. The mess is where the good stuff lives.

Bring this to our 90-minute session and we'll use it as the foundation for everything that comes next.

How to use this
01 Find a quiet hour. Pour something good. Let yourself think slowly.
02 Write in your own voice — the way you'd talk to a friend, not a brochure.
03 Skip anything that doesn't resonate. Come back to it later if you want.
04 The inspiration section at the end is just as important as the words — show me what draws you in.
01

Your work

1A
Tell me about what you do — in the way you'd explain it to someone at a dinner party, not in a pitch.
Don't worry about being concise. I want the real version, not the elevator version.
1B
Who do you work with? Describe the person who walks through your door — or the person you wish would.
Think about a specific client, real or imagined. What are they going through when they find you?
1C
How do people find you right now? And how do you wish they found you?
02

Your voice

2A
If your website could say one thing to the right person at the right moment, what would it be?
2B
What do you not want your site to sound like?
The things that make you cringe on other people's sites. The tone that would feel wrong for your work.
03

Your vision

3A
What do you want someone to do after visiting your site?
Book a call? Send an email? Follow you on Instagram? Buy something? There's no wrong answer — I just need to know where we're sending people.
3B
Where do you see your practice in a year?
This helps me build something that fits where you're going, not just where you are.
3C
What's been keeping you from having a website until now?
Be honest. This is useful information — it tells me where the stuckness lives so we can work with it, not around it.
04

Your inspiration

This is where you show me what draws you in. Don't overthink it — if something catches your eye, it belongs here. I'm looking for feel, not specifications.

4A
Sites you love
Paste 3–5 links to websites whose feel, look, or vibe speaks to you. They don't have to be in your industry. Just tell me what you like about each one.
4B
Images, colors, textures
Describe or paste links to images, color palettes, textures, or aesthetics you're drawn to. Pinterest boards, Instagram posts, photos on your phone — anything goes.
4C
Existing brand elements
Do you already have a logo, business name, colors, or fonts you love? A social media presence you want the site to match? List what exists — even if it's just "I like the color green and my business is called [name]."
4D
One feeling
If someone lands on your site and walks away with one feeling, what do you want it to be?

That's everything. Send this to me whenever you're ready — finished or unfinished, polished or messy. We'll use it as the starting point for our session together.

After you send, you'll see a copy of everything you wrote — save it, print it, or just know it's there.
