About

Hi, I'm Leah.

I've spent 25 years in tech — VP of Product, CPO, CPTO at startups, scale-ups, Amazon, and Google. I know how to build things that work.

I'm also a certified coach. ICF PCC, Certified Wayfinder Life Coach through MarthaBeck.com, Reiki Level II, EFT Tapping certification in progress, and a graduate of Witch School with Emma Griffin. I know how to sit with someone while they figure out what they're trying to say.

The Launch came from noticing that the practitioners in my world — coaches, healers, readers, somatic workers — kept getting stuck in the same place. They knew what they did. They just couldn't get it onto a page and into the world. The bottleneck was never technical. It was the crossing.

So I started building. First my own site, then a friend's, then a few more. Now it's a small studio — one to three builds a month, by appointment. I do the coaching work that helps people get clear, and then I build the thing that puts that clarity into public view.
